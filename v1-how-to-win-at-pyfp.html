How to Win at PYFP

01
Show Up
  • Log in 5 minutes early
  • Workbook and notes ready to go
  • Find a quiet space and shut out distractions
02
Camera On, Stay Present
  • Stay muted unless called on
  • Save questions for the group coaching sessions
  • When called on, open with "My question is…" No backstory. No life stories. Just the question.
03
Play Full Out
  • Be coachable
  • Implement in real time. Use this time to do the work.
  • No side coaching in chat. No self-promotion. No victim mindset.
  • What's shared here stays here.
